Bycrypt!

Bycrypt is a tool for generating encrypted QR codes.

Quick Start:
First, create your encrypted barcode here using your own configuration. Then scan it using any QR code scanner. You will receive a URL. Open the URL and enter your password. Your secured data will appear seemingly out of nowhere.

What you can do
  • Lock text, links, accounts, Wi-Fi details, contact cards, link lists, markdown notes and small files.
  • Unlock with a password, PIN, pattern, location or fingerprint, or with puzzles: icon sequences, picture hotspots, tile orders, melodies, rhythms, riddles and dial locks. Combine several for the best protection.
  • Legacy-compatible links open in every version of Bycrypt. Enhanced links add stronger encryption, compression, schedules, decoy keys and a privacy mode.
  • Batch many QR codes into a printable sheet, and inspect any link to see what it is made of.
